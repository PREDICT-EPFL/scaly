T3-4 and T3-5 · the generated interior-point solver (C-129) · branch claude/tier3-ipm · 26 Sep 2026

Summary. scaly.solvers.ipm.Solver is PIQP 0.6.2 as generated code, from Ruiz equilibration to restore_dual, with the dense and the sparse KKT backends. The tier gate is met.

  • Status parity: 62/62 problems, each backend against PIQP's run with the same backend (48 Maros–Mészáros, 5 infeasible, 2 MPC, 7 random).
  • Decision traces: sparse 48/48 and dense 47/48 on the problems where PIQP's own two backends agree. The other 14 are rounding-sensitive and are held to the status alone.
  • Against the reference: every iteration matches it at full precision, to 1e-12 … 1e-5, on eight well-conditioned problems.
  • Plan change: factorization retries and iterative refinement (#29) moved from Tier 4 into this PR. Without them, 9 of 55 problems ended as NUMERICS, because the condensed Cholesky fails along PIQP's path on 24 of the 48 stored problems.
  • One deviation, documented: the dense backend also fails a pivot that has no significant digit left, but only while refinement is off.
  • Speed: 1.96× PIQP's solve time with the sparse backend and 2.29× with the dense one (geometric means over 51 problems). That is T3-6's job.

Mutation checks: 19 of 20 killed; the survivor is equivalent up to rounding. Suite: see the commit.

What was built

  • Solver(structure, backend, settings).solve(values, trace=...) builds the whole solve as expressions.
    • The loop is one while_loop. Its carry holds the iterate, the proximal centres and PIQP's info scalars; the scaled data are loop params (C-112).
    • The step implements everything from #21 to #28: residuals and termination, initial point, Mehrotra predictor–corrector, fraction to boundary, proximal ρ/δ and centre updates, boundary shift, fine-tune switch, infeasibility detection, and the branch without inequalities.
    • It returns PIQP's result layout, status and iteration count. With trace it also returns PIQP's per-iteration table plus σ, and trace_rows, the rows PIQP prints: at the iteration limit, or after a failed factorization, the last pass prints none.
  • Kernels are the pieces the initial point and the loop share, each a Function built once per structure:
    • one factorization attempt inside PIQP's retry loop, a while_loop of at most 12 attempts;
    • the KKT solve;
    • PIQP's refinement: a first residual inside a one-pass loop, so nothing runs while refinement is off, then refinement steps that stop on tolerance or once the improvement rate drops below 5.
    The factorization appears once in the generated C.
  • SparseLDL.solve_with(factor, b) solves with a factor carried out of a loop.
  • Shared test helpers: problems.gate_problems(), piqp_trace.same_decisions and piqp_trace.backends_agree, now also used by the reference's own tests.

Why retries and refinement had to come now

The first gate run had no retries: 9 of 55 problems ended as NUMERICS on the dense backend. Replaying the reference's path showed that LAPACK's Cholesky of the condensed matrix fails at some iteration on 24 of the 48 stored problems (DUALC8 from factorization 4 on). PIQP gets through these by turning refinement on. Doing the same here fixed every status.

Late in a solve, a condensed Cholesky pivot is often rounding noise. On the reference's path, every LAPACK failure has a smallest relative pivot (pivot over diagonal entry) below 1.9e-16. The runs that LAPACK factored reach that level too: the 1% quantile is 2e-16.

Our Crout kernel sums in another order, so its noise pivots come out positive where LAPACK's come out negative. It never asked for refinement, and DUALC8 took 22 iterations instead of 10. The fix, while refinement is off, treats a pivot at or below ε times its diagonal entry as a failure: that catches 160 of LAPACK's 162 failures and flags 4 of its 451 successes. Once refinement is on, only PIQP's own test counts, so a noisy but positive pivot never forces a ×100 retry. With this, DUALC8 takes 10 iterations.

The dense backend's forward errors late in a solve match LAPACK's (1e-3 to 1e-1 relative, against SuperLU on the full KKT system). The remaining dense miss, QADLITTL, comes from a negative noise pivot on its last factorization with refinement on.

What the retry and refinement structure costs. An interleaved A/B against T3-3's direct factorization (a worktree of the last commit, minima, Spotlight busy) puts one factorization and two solves at +5–23%: sparse QSC205 25.4 → 31.3 µs, QCAPRI 86.7 → 100.3 µs, dense QAFIRO 13.6 → 16.5 µs. The cause is the factor copied through the retry loop's carry, about five copies per factorization (C-131).

Compile time. A cold first call takes 0.7–5.2 s, against 0.05–0.13 s for T3-3's factor-and-solve functions; the whole loop with its nested loops is now compiled.
