A. Batching and the product kernels (C-237, C-238)
--------------------------------------------------
C-237 held: row tiles exact against NumPy for every m from 1 to 80 on 18 targets, the panel form
at the M3's thresholds, tile_rows_max on 9 576 custom targets, the alignment's threshold and
where the attribute survives.

1. A body that multiplies by a constant of mostly zeros was batched into a dense product.
   const(C) @ x + x, loop us / batched us / ratio, before the fix:
     tridiagonal     n=64, 100 trips    5.74   24.07   4.19
                     n=128, 100 trips   9.24   86.52   9.36
                     n=160, 200 trips  16.55  253.00  15.29
     block diagonal  n=160, 200 trips  11.71  213.28  18.22
     dense (control) n=64 / 128 / 160   0.56 / 0.48 / 0.41
   After (a constant under a quarter full keeps the loop): 1.00 on every sparse row, the dense
   control unchanged (0.39 to 0.60).
2. A body with a rank-4 transpose no longer lowered on any target with lanes: batched, the
   transpose had five axes. After: the map keeps its loop, max error 3.6e-15 on four targets.
3. A batched product kept the procedure around the map in loops, where a sparse constant product
   beside it lost the folding of its zeros:
     sparse const 48, map of 6 trips   default 14.22 us, loop 5.93 us, 2.40
     sparse const 64, map of 8 trips   default 24.26 us, loop 6.58 us, 3.69
   After (lowering drops the batched form where it would end a procedure's expansion beside a
   product with a constant operand): 1.01 and 1.00.
4. A map with one output no trip changes kept the loop for it and batched the other output too:
     n=64, 50 trips   loop 22.86 us, batched 33.72 us, 1.48 (n=128: 1.50; n=96, 200 trips: 1.50)
   After (the outputs of one map are batched together or not at all): 1.00.
5. Four trips of a vector by a shared input matrix kept the loop, though lowering tiles four
   columns. Forced batching over the loop: 256 squared 0.57, 512 squared 0.49, 1024 squared 0.45;
   three trips 1.09 to 1.12, two trips 0.87 to 0.89. The other way, a product of four columns a
   trip was taken as not tiled and batched for nothing: 1.17 to 1.25.
   After (_tiled: at least four columns, as lowering tiles them): four trips are batched, 0.45 to
   0.57; three and two keep the loop; the four-column products keep the loop, 0.98 to 1.01.
6. "The same sum in the same order as in the loop" was not so: the loop's matrix times a vector
   adds four partial sums, a row of the batched product one. 49 of 81 entries differ at 9 x 8,
   272 of 360 at 40 x 33, by at most 7e-14 relative. The text is corrected, the code unchanged.
7. "Or when the shared matrix is larger than panel_bytes" overstated the rule, which also asks
   that the trips together fill tiles. The text is corrected.

B. One forward pass and the sparse derivatives (C-239, C-241)
-------------------------------------------------------------
About 4 000 random differential cases found no wrong result, pattern or C.

1. Regression: a callee with a reverse rule and an int64 or bool output the rule does not read
   could not be differentiated (a float zero passed for the output), in a call, a map and a scan
   body. After: a zero of the output's own type; 18 of 18 cases give the gradients.
2. hoist_invariant split one call a pass and analysed the whole body again after each:
     calls in one stage   100      200      400
     before the tier      0.01 s   0.02 s   0.07 s
     as committed         0.71 s   4.95 s   36.5 s
     after the fix        0.02 s   0.07 s   0.23 s
3. The cut at the selections built the whole derivative in the selections before comparing
   colors. A reduction over a gathered operand makes it dense in the entries gathered:
     Hessian, variable of 40, entries gathered   400      800      1600
     as committed                                0.87 s   6.1 s    60 s
     after the fix                               0.02 s   0.03 s   0.11 s
     Jacobian, variable of 500, three dense rows, entries gathered   8000              16000
     as committed                                                    3.7 s, 1.7 GB     14.5 s, 6.5 GB
     after the fix                                                   0.10 s, 226 MB    0.18 s, 386 MB
   The fix reads the pattern first: a row's entries take a color each, so a cut whose fullest
   row already holds as many entries as wrt needs colors is turned down before anything is built.
4. Lowering walked the whole graph once for each map to find its outputs:
     maps in one Function   500      1000     2000
     as committed           0.61 s   2.26 s   9.0 s
     after the fix          0.08 s   0.15 s   0.32 s
   Scans and while loops had the same walk before the tier; all three now share one.
5. The linear part folds coefficients in double precision when the graph is built. For
   x*1e200*1e200 - x*1e200*1e200 the generated code computes 0 and the folded coefficient was
   inf - inf: a Jacobian diagonal of NaN where it had been 0. A division by a constant with a
   zero warned at build. After: a coefficient that is not finite leaves the expression whole, and
   the folding does not warn.
6. Two statements in the documentation: a call in the body of a while_loop is not split by the
   hoist (the text said every call with partly invariant arguments); under vmap it is the
   Function's outputs the rule reads that come from the map, not "the rule's outputs".
