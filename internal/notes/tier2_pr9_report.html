Tier 2 · PR 9 report

T2-9 · schedules, refinement, health checks, options and sparsity overrides; the two non-PIQP examples (C-110) · branch claude/tier2-sparse · 26 Sep 2026

Summary. This PR finishes the Tier 2 feature list.

  • Schedules. SparseLDL can now emit straight-line code (schedule="unroll") or column loops ("scan"). "auto" unrolls up to sc.options(sparse_unroll=1000) operations. On small KKT systems straight-line code factors 5–8× faster than the loops and 4–5× faster than the C baseline.
  • Refinement. solve(b, refine=k, tol=None) refines a fixed number of times, or adaptively in a while_loop. Both run inside the implicit derivative rules. At δ = 10⁻¹⁰, two steps take the residual from 2·10⁻⁵ to 4·10⁻¹⁵.
  • Health. inertia() and health(signs=, pivot_tol=, x=) are computed in the generated code.
  • Generic compiler pieces (T2.f):
    • sc.options(dense_unroll=, sparse_unroll=, max_trajectory=);
    • the dense unroll decision stored in the graph;
    • custom_derivative(sparsity=), honoured everywhere a Function is applied;
    • a memory guard on reverse passes through loops.
  • Sparsity analysis. Two improvements came out of benchmarking:
    • loops that read nothing depending on wrt are skipped (5.1 s → 16 ms for a solve's pattern);
    • a crash on scan carries of 256 or more entries is fixed.
  • Examples. An SQP Newton step with a sparse KKT system and inertia correction converges quadratically. A Kalman update with a sparse information prior matches the covariance form, and its Jacobian is the Kalman gain.

Suite: 1 421 passed, 88 skipped (1 365 before); the same four VM-only failures. ruff and ty are clean. Mutation checks: 28 of 28 killed; five only after a test was added.

1. What was built

PieceDetails
Options (utils/options.py)
  • dense_unroll (8), sparse_unroll (1000) and max_trajectory (50 M). Each is a non-negative int, validated at the call.
  • Like nonsmooth, each is read when a graph is built. cholesky, ldl and solve_triangular nodes now carry an unroll attribute, which the lowering reads. The choice is therefore part of the graph and of the JIT cache key.
Straight-line sparse LDLᵀ
  • SparseLDL(schedule="unroll") runs the same left-looking algorithm in Python at build time, over the same symbolic tables. It builds one scalar expression per entry of L and D, and the two sweeps the same way.
  • The factor layout [L | D], the solver Function and its rules are shared with the loop schedule.
  • fact.work = update multiply-adds + nnz(L) is what "auto" compares with the threshold.
Refinement
  • refine=k without tol gives k steps x += K⁻¹(b − Kx), unrolled in the graph.
  • With tol, a while_loop of at most k steps runs while ‖b − Kx‖∞ > tol·max(1, ‖b‖∞). Its carry is [f | K | b | x | r | threshold], because a loop body reads only its carry.
  • The refined solve is the body of the custom_derivative, so tangents and cotangents are refined solves too. One solver Function is kept per (refine, tol), and the sweep bodies are built once per factorization.
Health
  • inertia() is (#D > 0, #D < 0, rest) as float64.
  • health() is a bool: every pivot is finite with |D| > pivot_tol. With signs (in K's own order, permuted internally), it tests signs·D > pivot_tol. With x, the solution must also be finite.
Sparsity override (T2.f)
  • custom_derivative(fn, ..., sparsity=f), where f(output, input) returns a SparsityType, a mask, a SciPy matrix or None.
  • A new _callee_mask is used by calls, vmap, scan and while_loop bodies, and by the structured sparse Jacobian of a vmap. Copies inherit the override.
  • SparseLDL's solve declares the per-connected-component pattern. It depends on K's entries only where the factorization reads them (lower entries of mirrored pairs), and not on the factor. It is built lazily.
Trajectory guard (T2.f)Reverse mode through a scan or while_loop refuses to store more than max_trajectory values. The error names the loop body and points to custom_derivative.
Sparsity analysis fixes
  • Call, vmap, scan and while patterns return early when no argument depends on wrt. The pattern of a solve in b used to walk every factorization loop, even though the factor does not depend on b.
  • The scan-cycle key stored the shape with bytes(shape), which fails for any dimension of 256 or more.
T2.g (assessed)
  • Multi-tensor carries and an int64 carry spill are not needed:
    • index tables are constants sliced per step;
    • every carry so far is float64;
    • an integer in a carry is exact up to 2⁵³.
  • What is missing is loop-invariant inputs for while_loop, which would remove the adaptive refinement's carry copies (C-112).
Examples
  • examples/sqp_newton_sparse.py: a pendulum OCP in multiple-shooting form (N = 30, 92 variables, 62 constraints).
    • The Hessian and Jacobian are sparse (sparse_hessian(triangle="lower"), sparse_jacobian), and the KKT matrix is assembled with SparseMatrix.block.
    • Each step uses SparseLDL with one refinement step, and inertia() drives an inertia-correcting rho.
  • examples/kalman_update.py: a 200-point field with a pentadiagonal information prior and 25 local-average sensors.
    • The update solves the quasi-definite system [[Λ, Hᵀ], [H, −R]].
    • jacobian(x, z) is the Kalman gain, and jacobian(x, r) comes from the implicit rule.

Inertia, not signs, for SQP. The first version of the example checked signs·D > 0, and Newton then stalled at ρ = 0.1. The Hessian of a nonconvex Lagrangian is legitimately indefinite. What the step needs is the right inertia ((n, m, 0), by Sylvester's law read off D), and with that check Newton converges quadratically at ρ = 0:

iteration 0  ||KKT residual|| = 4.0e+01  rho = 1e-04   (H is singular at the start)
iteration 1                     5.9e-01        0
iteration 2                     4.5e-02        0
iteration 3                     2.8e-04        0
iteration 4                     5.8e-09        0
iteration 5                     8.9e-16        0

signs= remains the right check for a quasi-definite matrix, which is what PIQP's regularized KKT systems are.

2. Tests

  • tests/linalg/test_sparse_ldl.py (50 → 96 cases):
    • the whole factor-and-solve matrix, with 3 matrices × 4 orderings × 3 triangles, and the implicit-derivative test now run for both schedules;
    • the unrolled code has no loop procedures;
    • "auto" switches at exactly work;
    • refinement reaches the rounding level, and tol=1e30 takes no step;
    • step counts with an inexact factor: refining against K with the factor of 1.2 K contracts the error by exactly 1/6, so k steps give (1 − 6^−(k+1)) K⁻¹b, and the adaptive loop's count (3 for ‖b‖ = 100, 1 for ‖b‖ = 0.01) can be read off the result;
    • gradients of refined solves equal those of plain ones;
    • inertia and health on a KKT system, including NaN and ∞ entries, a singular matrix, an infinite pivot, wrong signs, pivot_tol, and a non-finite x;
    • on a two-block system: the per-component pattern, the entries-read pattern, and a sparse Jacobian that colors to 9 and equals K⁻¹.
  • tests/ad/test_sparsity.py (+6): the override through call, vmap, scan and while_loop; the three pattern forms and None; the wrong-shape error; a 300-entry scan carry; a loop independent of wrt.
  • tests/utils/test_options.py (+2, one extended):
    • validation of the three counts;
    • dense_unroll per op (the attribute, loops or no loops, equal values);
    • the trajectory guard for scan and while_loop at exactly the limit.
  • tests/linalg/test_examples.py (2):
    • SQP: quadratic convergence, inertia correction at the start only, and a fixed point at the solution.
    • Kalman: the mean, the gain and ∂x/∂r = −K diag(S⁻¹ν) against the dense covariance form.

Mutation checks (28 of 28 killed).

  • Unrolled factor and solve: update sign, backward sweep, and the diagonal division.
  • Refinement: fixed count, loop condition, max_iter, the max(1, ·) threshold, and the residual update.
  • Health: isfinite, the x check, the sign permutation, pivot_tol, and the inertia's <.
  • Schedule: the auto threshold and the definition of work.
  • Solve pattern: the entries-read mask and the factor's None.
  • Override: ignored in vmap, scan and while; the shape check; None as dense.
  • Other: the 256-byte key; the guard's comparison and the guard missing from while; the unroll attribute ignored in the factor and in the solve lowering; bool accepted as a count.

Five first survived:

  • fixed count, max_iter and threshold: refinement against an exact factor converges in one step, so the step count was invisible;
  • isfinite: an ∞ entry turned into NaN pivots;
  • the factor's unroll attribute: one test covered all three ops.

Each gained the test above that catches it.

4. Tier 2 gates

  • Accuracy against SciPy across δ ∈ {10⁻⁴, 10⁻⁸, 10⁻¹⁰, 10⁻¹³}: met (PR 7). Refinement now reaches residuals at the rounding level at δ = 10⁻¹⁰.
  • Generated LDLᵀ at most 2× QDLDL-class C: met. The loops are within 1.04–1.37× (PR 8); straight-line code is below 1× on small systems.
  • Generation linear in nnz(L), under 10 s at 50 k: met for the loops (0.8 s at 46 k). Straight-line code is linear in the work and capped by sparse_unroll.
  • Dense kernels at most 2× LAPACK at n = 32–64: met at -O3; open at gcc -O2 (C-107, your decision).
  • Two non-PIQP examples: met (SQP Newton step, Kalman update).
