Interpolation · SP2 report

API-172 · shape-preserving kinds, smooth_linear, smoothing splines, spline calculus, the inverse · branch claude/interp · 28 Sep 2026 · plan interp_plan_2026_09_28.md

Summary. The kinds whose fits are not a plain interpolation, and what can be done with a spline besides evaluating it.

  • Five new kinds: pchip, akima, makima equal SciPy's to 1e-13; steffen equals the paper's formulas, and on 10⁴ random monotone data sets it is monotone and never leaves an interval's range; smooth_linear equals CasADi's to 1e-15, its algorithm read from CasADi's source.
  • interp.smoothing: P-splines of any degree and dimension, gridded or scattered, λ by generalized cross-validation; method="cubic" is make_smoothing_spline. At 20 000 points the P-spline takes 9 ms; SciPy's own GCV gives up there.
  • Calculus: derivative and antiderivative as splines on the same partition (the derivative shares the spline's search), integrate exact in every extrapolation mode, and inverse() of a strictly monotone curve by safeguarded Newton with dx/dy = 1/f' as its derivative.

Suite: 2 824 passed, 45 skipped. ruff clean; ty clean on src/ and the new tests. Mutation checks: 26 of 26 killed, five only after a test was added; one guard found dead and removed.

1. What was built

PieceDetails
Shape-preserving kinds (fit.py)
  • Slopes by SciPy's formulas operation for operation (PCHIP's weighted harmonic mean and Moler's end rule; Akima's extrapolated secants, the 1e-9 break and makima's weights) and by Steffen (1990) eqs. 11 and 26–27.
  • Each is a C¹ cubic on knots with every interior site doubled: the B-coefficients are the Bézier points y_i ∓ h d_i / 3, so the evaluation, search, derivatives and calculus are the ones every spline has. 1-D only: in n-D they are not tensor products (API-185).
smooth_linearFrom casadi/solvers/bspline_interpolant.hpp: a cubic with knots at each site and at ± frac · the smallest spacing around it, coefficients the linear interpolant at the Greville points (summed in CasADi's order). Linear away from the sites, rounded within frac of them, not through the data.
interp.smoothingP-spline: uniform knots running k past the data (the base interval exactly the data's range), the design matrix as a row-wise product of the axes' (SciPy's NdBSpline.design_matrix sizes its columns by the last one used), difference penalties per axis, a Cholesky solve; GCV on a coarse grid of log10 λ then a bounded search.
Calculus (spline.py)
  • derivative(nu, axis): de Boor differencing. Outside, the derivative of the extrapolation: linear gives the end slope held, clamp and fill give zero. It equals differentiating the spline's graph at every point but the knots, in every mode.
  • antiderivative(axis): zero at the base interval's start (for unclamped knots too, as a P-spline's), and the antiderivative of the extrapolation where that is a mode (clamp → linear, zero fill → clamp).
  • integrate(a, b): numbers give a number, exact in every mode in 1-D (clamped, linear and filled tails in closed form, periodic by SciPy), inside the box in n-D; Expr bounds go through the antiderivative, 2^D corners in n-D.
  • inverse(): monotonicity checked exactly (the derivative's sign at the cell ends and at its own extrema). Degree 1 gives the swapped table; above, an Inverse: Newton from the linear table's guess on the cell it names, bisecting when a step would leave the cell, in a while_loop; custom_derivative supplies 1/f', so Hessians work and never reach the iterations. Outside the range it continues along the end tangent, and holds a flat end.
Along the wayAxis builds in linear time for sorted knots (no np.unique/np.isin sorts); its local tables are extracted from the sparse basis without densifying it (a (cells × n) dense array ran a 10⁶-site fit out of memory); auto strategy compares the two tables' sizes, since basis stores (k+1)² values per cell in 1-D against pp's k+1; spline fits call make_interp_spline once.
