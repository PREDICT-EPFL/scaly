Interpolation · SP0 report

C-170 · a zero derivative for floor and ceil, forward mode through integer index arithmetic, the feasibility spike · branch claude/interp · 28 Sep 2026 · plan interp_plan_2026_09_28.md

Summary. The compiler changes the interpolation package relies on, and the measurement behind the plan's kill criterion.

  • floor and ceil differentiate. Their derivative is zero, exact off the jumps. A periodic wrap sin(x − T floor(x/T)) gives the gradient, Jacobian and Hessian of sin at the wrapped point to 1e-14. nonsmooth="error" still refuses them.
  • Integer index arithmetic in forward mode. An index vector built by broadcast int64 arithmetic (arange(4) + [cell]·4, or a batch's (N,1)·4 + (1,4)) now differentiates under sc.jacobian, sc.hessian, sc.jvp and sc.sparse_jacobian. It failed on a mixed-dtype product.
  • Spike. A spline evaluation made of existing ops runs in 0.06× (1-D cubic, 1 000 knots), 0.03× (2-D bicubic, 64²) and 0.39× (3-D trilinear, 20³) the time of CasADi's generated interpolant, with a tenth of the C lines and lowering under 0.05 s. The dedicated op of §10 is not needed on these numbers; SP4 re-measures with the library.

Suite: 2 652 passed, 45 skipped (baseline 2 644 and 45). Mutation checks: 9 of 9 killed, one only after the clamp test was strengthened.

1. What was built

PieceDetails
ir/expr.pyFLOOR and CEIL become differentiable in OP_INFO, as minimum did when its tie convention arrived, so the type says a derivative (zero) flows and the AD rules decide it. cast's docstring states that a non-finite or out-of-range float cast to an integer is undefined in the generated C, and that clamping with minimum/maximum first leaves a finite value.
ad/forward.py
  • floor_tangent: zero, after _nonsmooth_mode, which raises under "error". Used by the single-seed JVP, the multi-seed structural JVP and reverse mode.
  • _jvp and _jvp_many_structural give a node of integer or bool dtype a zero tangent before dispatching on its op. The float tangent of an index cannot combine with the int64 arithmetic above it, and has no meaning anyway. The guard is on dtype rather than on diff=False: a float node with diff=False keeps today's behaviour.
ad/reverse.pyThe VJP of floor/ceil is floor_tangent's zero.
ad/sparsity.pyNo dependence through floor/ceil, so a Jacobian pattern carries no structural entry that is always zero. Under "error" the dependence is kept, so that sparse_jacobian still reaches the refusal instead of silently skipping it.
Docsdocs/guide/options.md and the Options docstring gain the floor/ceil line.

3. Spike

perf_2026_09_28_interp/bench_spike.py. It uses a prototype, not the library: a branch-free binary search (1-D, 10 halvings on knots padded with +inf) or the uniform search with its one-step correction (2-D, 3-D); one take of the cell's coefficients; Horner. CasADi 3.8's interpolant is code-generated at each lookup mode and the faster is kept. One point per call, 7 rounds.

  • CasADi refuses lookup_mode="exact" for a bspline even on an exactly uniform grid. It applies the mode to the B-spline's knot vector, which not-a-knot leaves unequally spaced. Its linear mode was slower than binary at 64 knots.
  • The bicubic's piecewise-polynomial table makes 1.3 MB of C source against CasADi's 0.32 MB: 16 values per cell against about one per point. SP1's basis strategy is the lean form, and SP4 sets the storage budget above which auto uses it.
