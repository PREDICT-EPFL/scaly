The spline kinds fit once (a first version called `make_interp_spline` a second time for the knots

`make_smoothing_spline` takes 30 ms at 200 points and 312 ms at 2 000, and fails its own GCV at 20 000
("ill-posed").

## SP4 thresholds

- `DENSE_FIT = 40` (from 256): the in-graph cubic fit's value and gradient take 33 / 150 ns (dense
  map / scans) at 16 sites, 283 / 375 at 32, 1 829 / 692 at 64, 11 804 / 1 571 at 128.
- `PP_BUDGET = 4 MB` (from 1 MB): per-cell polynomials stay faster at random points even at 8.4 MB
  (above), but cost 21.5 MB of C and 1.1 s of compilation against 1.5 MB and 0.1 s.
- `LARGE_TABLE = 2^20` values: the warning's threshold, about 20 MB of C.

## The dedicated-op gate (plan section 10)

On the gate's configurations the composite evaluation is far inside every limit: the 2-D 64 x 64
bicubic's value and gradient take 0.035x CasADi's time at its fastest (1.5x allowed), with 0.08x
its C lines (3x allowed) and 0.06 s of lowering (1 s allowed); the 3-D 20^3 trilinear value takes
0.36x, 0.07x and 0.04 s. `ExprOp.SPLINE_EVAL` (API-179) is not needed.
