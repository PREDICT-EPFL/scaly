## SP2 fitting cost (`bench_fit.py`, Python wall time, fastest of 3)

| kind | n = 100 | n = 1e4 | n = 1e6 | SciPy constructor at 1e6 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| linear | 0.04 ms | 0.23 | 26 | 0.8 |
| cubic | 0.09 | 1.4 | 158 | 14.9 |
| spline (k = 5) | 0.09 | 1.6 | 183 | 87 |
| pchip | 0.06 | 0.40 | 37 | 8.9 |
| akima | 0.06 | 0.36 | 36 | 10.9 |
| steffen | 0.06 | 0.37 | 37 | — |
| smooth_linear | 0.11 | 1.4 | 170 | — |

The spline kinds fit once (a first version called `make_interp_spline` a second time for the knots
alone: 216 ms for the cubic at 1e6); the rest over SciPy is the checks and the partition's merge of
sites and knots, all O(n log n) at most.

The first evaluation traced adds the per-cell tables: 1 ms at 100 sites, 7 to 16 ms at 1e4, 0.2 to
1.6 s at 1e6. P-spline smoothing with GCV: 1.8 ms (200 points, 20 segments) to 15 ms (20 000, 80);
`make_smoothing_spline` takes 30 ms at 200 points and 312 ms at 2 000, and fails its own GCV at 20 000
("ill-posed").
