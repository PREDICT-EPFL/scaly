Interpolation · SP3 report

API-173 · coefficients and data as expressions · branch claude/interp · 28 Sep 2026 · plan interp_plan_2026_09_28.md

Summary. A table read at run time, or fitted as decision variables.

  • BSpline(knots, c) with an Expr c: evaluated from the local bases, its Function taking the coefficients as an input (broadcast to every point of a batch), with derivative, antiderivative and integrate as constant maps of the coefficients.
  • interpolant(grid, data) with Expr data, every kind: the fits linear in the data as constant maps (sparse for smooth_linear), a C² cubic above 256 sites by a tridiagonal (or cyclic) solve in two scans, the shape-preserving kinds by their slope formulas as expressions; smoothing with a given λ. All equal the NumPy fits to 1e-13.
  • basis(points) and at(points) for points known now: the design matrix with the spline's extrapolation, and the spline as one sparse product whose Jacobian has exactly the basis's pattern. At 2 000 points and 100 coefficients the Jacobian costs 5.7 µs through at(), 351 µs at symbolic points, and 22.5 ms in CasADi inlined.

Suite: 2 860 passed, 45 skipped. ruff and ty clean on src/ and the new tests. Mutation checks: 16 of 16 killed, ten only after a test was added or fixed.

1. What was built

PieceDetails
spline.py
  • Expr coefficients force the basis strategy (pp tabulates constants). function() is then (x, c) → f(x), interned by the axes and the coefficients' shape; a batch maps it with the coefficients at stride zero, which the hoisting pass moves out of the loop.
  • _along applies a constant matrix along an axis to either kind of coefficients: NumPy, or a constant product (through SparseMatrix when sparse) with the axis moved to the front and back.
  • integrate with numeric bounds is w·c + offset, the basis functions' integrals in every extrapolation mode and a fill's contribution; the Expr antiderivative is the cumulative sum less its value at the start as one map.
  • basis uses Axis.local_basis, the NumPy twin of the run-time evaluation (wrap, clamp, linear continuation, fill), multiplied out across axes; at is its sparse product plus the fill values.
fit.py
  • A linear kind's map is the fit of the identity (make_interp_spline of eye(n)), applied along the axis; smooth_linear's is its sparse Greville interpolation.
  • Above DENSE_FIT = 256 sites a C² cubic solves SciPy's own slope system (not-a-knot, natural and clamped end rows; periodic by Sherman and Morrison) by the Thomas algorithm, pivots and multipliers found now, a forward and a backward scan, and is represented on doubled knots like the Hermite kinds.
  • The PCHIP, Akima, makima and Steffen formulas run on NumPy arrays or expressions through one small table of operations. Every division reads 1 in the branch it does not take, so reverse mode never forms 0·∞; at a kink in the data the derivative follows sc.options(nonsmooth=...).
  • smoothing with Expr data and a given λ: the P-spline's or the smoothing spline's map; "gcv" needs the data's values and is refused.

2. Tests and mutation checks

  • tests/integration/test_interp_ocp.py (+1): a 6×5 table calibrated to 300 noisy measurements by IPOPT, through at() and through a batch evaluation, equal to the least-squares solution to 1e-8.
