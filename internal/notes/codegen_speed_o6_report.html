O6: output rows in register blocks

Step 6 of the generated-code speed plan · todo C-201 · branch perf/codegen-speed · 30 Sep 2026 · Apple M3 Max, Apple clang 21, the JIT's flags

Summary. x @ b and a @ b put the reduction loop outermost (C-43) so that each output sums its terms in order and the inner loop runs over independent outputs. The price was the output row itself: every step of k loaded and stored all of it. Reverse mode through a dense layer is exactly this product (adjoint @ W), and forward mode's multi-seed tangents are the matrix-matrix one. A row of up to 64 columns now runs in blocks of 16, 8 and 4 columns whose sums stay in registers across k, each output stored once, summed in the same order.

  • Speed: matmul_48 2.13× faster (12.6 → 5.9 µs), mlp_small_jac 1.15×, npmpc 1.04×; unbumpercars 1.01× slower (489 → 495 µs by a direct A/B, from one 256 × 6 product).
  • Bounds, from the measurements: the one to three columns no block covers, and rows wider than 64, keep the reduction outermost. One to three chains of a long reduction are slower than the memory traffic they save (256 × 2: 1.47×), and the blocks, reading the matrix a column block at a time, were 1.13× slower on the unbumpercars oracle's 128 × 256 products though a hand-written 128 × 256 was faster in isolation.
  • Order: every output sums over k in order, as before. The result can still differ in the last place where the compiler fuses a multiply-add differently in the new loop shape (seen once, a single leftover column).
  • Tests: 4 449 passed, 57 skipped, 5 xfailed (19 new). Six mutants, six killed (one after widening a test case). Snapshot wide.c regenerated.
  • Tried first and dropped: two interleaved lanes inside each quarter of the dense Cholesky tile's dot products (the headroom -fassociative-math shows there, 0.70 on chol_solve_200): 1.45× slower. Clang did not pair the even and odd sums into vector lanes, and 32 scalar sums with 16 loaded values spill the 32 registers. It needs explicit vector types in the program dialect.
  • Undo: git revert the commit “Codegen speed O6”.

What changed

  • passes/lowering.py: _lower_columns_blocked lowers vec @ mat and mat @ mat (the latter row by row); _mm_accumulate, now unused, is gone. COLUMN_BLOCKS = (16, 8, 4), COLUMN_BLOCKED_MAX = 64.
  • docs/how_it_works/lowering.md: the matrix products' forms.

A block wider than the columns it has sends them all down the single-chain rest, which is why the widths cascade (16s, then one 8, then one 4) and why fewer than four columns keep the old form.
