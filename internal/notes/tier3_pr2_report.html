Summary.

  • The first piece of the generated IPM. scaly.solvers.ipm contains:
    • QPStructure, the static patterns and bound finiteness;
    • QPValues, PIQP's preprocessing: free rows zeroed and bounded by ±1, finite box bounds packed;
    • ruiz and scale.
    Ruiz is a while_loop (T3-1's params hold the data) that reproduces PIQP's passes, limit_scaling, the 1e-3 stopping test and cost scaling with its aliasing.
  • Exact. Scalings, scaled P and c, and the box scaling match the reference to 1e-13 on all 48 stored problems, with and without cost scaling, on the infeasible set and on MPC.
  • C-125, a silent wrong-number bug in codegen. An output named like an input, as in examples/simple.py, had its buffer replace the input's, so the input was read from the output buffer. Fixed in lowering and in the C entry, with 4 tests, and committed separately.

Suite: 1979 passed. Mutation checks: C-125 2 of 2 killed. T3-2 is checked by exact comparison: any change to a scaling rule moves a scaling on some of the 48 problems far beyond 1e-13.
