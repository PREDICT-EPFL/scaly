Integrators · I3 report

API-144 · implicit Runge-Kutta integrators · branch claude/integrators-mpc · 27 Sep 2026 · plan integrators_mpc_plan_2026_09_27.md

Summary. si.implicit discretizes stiff models with collocation-grade accuracy.

  • Methods.
    • Four families for any number of stages: Gauss-Legendre, Radau IIA, Lobatto IIIA and IIIC.
    • Five named methods: backward Euler, implicit midpoint, trapezoidal, SDIRK2 and SDIRK3.
    • Every tableau is checked against the order conditions. Each has exactly its textbook order, and its textbook A- and L-stability.
  • Newton inside the generated code. Iterations are fixed or run to a tolerance, simplified or full. Simplified Newton on a coupled method uses RADAU5's split of I − h A⊗J by the eigenvalues of A, and a diagonally implicit method solves stage by stage.
  • Derivatives by the implicit function theorem. They are exact in every mode and to second order, including forward over forward, and never go through the iterations.
  • Against CasADi SX (the same Radau IIA(3) step, written out so it can be code-generated), on the cart-pole:
    • the step takes 0.62× the time and the step Jacobians 0.94×;
    • the generated code is a fifth of CasADi's for the step and an eighteenth for the Jacobians.
  • An AD change on the way. A call that reaches a custom forward rule now takes the joint multi-seed pass. The rule used to run once per seed, redoing its factorization each time. That change alone cut the step Jacobian from 3.76 to 2.58 µs.

Suite: 2 578 passed, 42 skipped. The one failure is Colin's uncommitted tiny_qp gallery test. ruff and ty are clean. Mutation checks: 24 of 24 killed; three only after a test was added, and one redundant check was deleted.

1. What was built

PieceDetails
integrators/polynomial.py (new)
  • Gauss, right Radau and Lobatto nodes on [0, 1]. Each starts from Legendre series roots, refined by Newton to rounding.
  • lagrange_integrals, the integrals of the Lagrange basis by a Gauss rule exact for its degree.
  • I5 extends this module with differentiation and interpolation matrices.
integrators/tableau.py
  • Collocation families from their nodes. gauss_legendre(s), radau_iia(s) and lobatto_iiia(s) take a_ij = ∫₀^{c_i} ℓ_j and b_j = ∫₀¹ ℓ_j.
  • Lobatto IIIC uses Chipman's construction: a_i1 = b_1, plus C(s−1).
  • Alexander's SDIRK2 and SDIRK3. For SDIRK3, γ is the root of the cubic.
  • Lookup. FAMILIES and tableau(name, stages). The property diagonally_implicit selects stage-by-stage Newton.
  • Order checks. Tree generation takes 2 ms up to order 11, so every tableau up to Gauss(5), order 10, is checked in full.
integrators/implicit.py (new)
  • _Newton. Fixed iterations are unrolled. With a tolerance, the iterations are a while_loop whose carry holds the iterate and its residual, so each iteration evaluates the residual once. The factors are loop parameters.
  • _Linear / _EigenSplit. A = T L T⁻¹ is computed once in NumPy, with L in real block form. The Newton system then decouples:
    • I − hγJ for each real eigenvalue γ;
    • a 2n×2n real block for each complex pair;
    • nothing for a zero eigenvalue (Lobatto IIIA's explicit stage).
    Radau IIA(3) factors a 4×4 and an 8×8 system instead of one 12×12. It falls back to the whole system when cond(T) > 1e6: the families stay below 3·10³ up to seven stages, while a Jordan block reads 3·10⁸.
  • Stage-by-stage solving for diagonally implicit methods. Simplified SDIRK factors one I − hγJ(x) shared by every stage.
  • The stage Function (x, others…, [h], K*) → K is the identity on K*, carrying custom_derivative rules:
    • forward, dK = −solve(G_K, G_x dx + …), with G's partial derivatives taken at a free K and then substituted;
    • reverse, one transposed solve and a vjp of G;
    • K*'s tangent is never read and its cotangent is zero, so the iterations are never differentiated.
  • Two levels of forward rules, as in SparseLDL.solve. G_K is built from one model Jacobian per stage.
integrators/model.pyThe method contract is now (model, name, h) → step, with h the substep length or None. A method builds its Functions (the stage Function, its rules) once per map, and the step receives the model's other leaves explicitly.
ad/forward.py_reaches_custom_jvp. A call whose callee reaches a custom forward rule, directly or through callees, is differentiated in the joint multi-seed pass, as a loop-bearing callee already was. The rule is then a vmap over the seeds, and hoist_invariant moves what the seeds share out of it (the stage Jacobians and the factorization of G_K). Before, a constant seed took the per-seed path, which applied the rule once per seed.
DocsAn implicit-methods section in docs/guide/integrators.md (methods, Newton, derivatives), and the API page entries.

2. Tests and mutation checks

There are 90 new tests across test_polynomial.py, test_tableau.py, test_implicit.py and tests/ad/test_joint_jvp.py.

  • Nodes. Quadrature degree 2s−1, 2s−2 and 2s−3 for s up to 8; the Lobatto nodes are symmetric.
  • Tableaus.
    • Every family and stage count from 1 to 5 has exactly its order.
    • Stability: |R(iy)| ≤ 1 and |R(−x)| ≤ 1 over nine decades, with R(−∞) = 0 exactly for the L-stable methods.
    • Stiff accuracy; Gauss's symplecticity condition; the known Radau IIA(2) and Lobatto IIIC(2) tableaus; the aliases.
  • Convergence.
    • Twelve methods converge at their order on a linear system with expm as the reference. One Newton iteration is exact there.
    • Radau IIA(3) on Van der Pol agrees with DOP853 at 1e-13, as does solve_ivp(Radau).
  • Stiffness. On Prothero-Robinson with hλ = −2·10⁴, the L-stable methods track the smooth solution to 1e-7 or better. RK4 overflows within three steps.
  • Derivatives. Eight Newton variants: coupled, full, Lobatto, SDIRK, SDIRK full, tolerance loops and looped substeps. Each is checked for:
    • Jacobians in x, u and dt;
    • gradients;
    • Hessians against finite differences of the gradient;
    • forward over forward.
    Also: a fixed dt with substeps; and the implicit derivative equals AD through eight hand-written full Newton iterations, to 1e-10.
  • Structure.
    • The LU orders are {2, 4} for the split Radau IIA(3), {6} with full Newton, {2} for SDIRK, and one LU for all SDIRK stages.
    • The eigen-split reconstructs A for six tableaus and refuses a Jordan block, whose method then factors the whole system.
    • Split and whole-system Newton take the same iterate.
  • AD. A call reaching a custom rule maps the rule over the seeds as one vmap, and _reaches_custom_jvp looks through callees.

How the Radau IIA(3) row got there, step / Jacobians in ns:

  1. First version: 882 / 4 805. One 12×12 system, factored in loops.
  2. Eigen-split: 371 / 3 761. A 4×4 and an 8×8 system, both straight-line code.
  3. Unrolling the rule's 12×12 LU: 371 / 3 756. This showed the factorization was not the cost, and the change was not kept.
  4. A profile showed 60% of the time in the forward rule, called once per seed with everything recomputed.
  5. The joint pass: 371 / 2 580. The rule is mapped over the seeds and its shared work hoisted.

What remains in the Jacobian is the hoisted prologue (30%), the per-seed 12×12 solves (12%) and the model's tangents.
