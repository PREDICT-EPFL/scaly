PIQP in Scaly

Every operation needed to implement PIQP in Scaly, ordered from most generally useful to PIQP-specific · 25 Sep 2026

Scope and assumptions

  • Target: the sparse PIQP algorithm (proximal interior point, Mehrotra predictor–corrector, Ruiz scaling, LDLᵀ), written once in Python and emitted as C specialised to a fixed sparsity pattern. Runtime index arrays are out of scope.
  • No CVXGEN-style unrolling: all loops are real loops (scan/while). The whole symbolic phase runs in NumPy at generation time, and the numeric phase is dataflow over constant index tables.
  • Keep Expr pure. Loops take a functional carry, and in-place carry donation happens at lowering.
  • Checked against the tree: ExprOp has elementwise minimum/maximum, abs, sum, gather, scatter (which rejects repeated indices even though its docstring says they accumulate), call, vmap. DType already defines bool_, int32, int64, but no op produces them and the ABI is double-only. The program dialect has for/range, store, load, mod, but no while/if/compare. C-54 (memory-ordered loads) is deferred.

Suggested milestones

  1. M1 — primitives (#1–5, #9). Each is a small, self-contained Expr op with AD, lowering and a NumPy reference test. They are immediately useful for contact models and diagnostics.
  2. M2 — loops (#6–8). scan first, then while_loop. C-54 becomes mandatory here. Gate: a Kalman filter or rollout with no unrolling, matching the unrolled result.
  3. M3 — sparse LDLᵀ (#11–18). Gate: residual against scipy.sparse on random quasi-definite KKTs and the MPC benchmark KKTs; render time linear in nnz.
  4. M4 — a plain Mehrotra QP (#19–25) with fixed regularisation. This is already a usable generated QP solver.
  5. M5 — PIQP proper (#26–32). Gate: iterate-by-iterate agreement with vendored PIQP; timing vs vendored PIQP on the benchmarks. Add #10 for sensitivities.
