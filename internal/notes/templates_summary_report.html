Function templates and multi-parameter functions: summary

2026-09-27 · branch claude/function-templates (from claude/ipm-speed) · todo API-1 · plan function_templates_plan_2026_09_27.md (v2)

Summary. A decorated body now takes one argument per parameter, declares shapes by value or not at all, and is instantiated once per argument signature under a deterministic C name. Derivatives work over templates too. The whole tree moved to the new convention: tests, plugins, examples, notebooks, benchmarks and docs. The C snapshots are byte-identical, and concrete calls are faster than before (2.83 to 2.45 µs for one leaf). The suite grew from 2348 to 2403 tests, all passing.

How it was done

Five review agents read the plan against the code: typing under the pinned ty, frontend semantics, compiler consumers, migration, and the user's view. The plan's settled decisions stood, and the review changed the design under them:

  • ConcreteFunction subclasses Function, so there is no wrapper, no delegation table and no extra call frame;
  • zero-argument calls evaluate, with no trace-depth context variable;
  • keywords are bound only when passed;
  • shapes and names are declarations;
  • wrt and of have defaults;
  • P1b was split in two, and P5 deferred.

Plan §10 lists all of it. Each phase is one commit with its own report, mutation checks and gates.

Numbers

  • Tests: 2348 to 2403 passing, 42 skipped throughout. New files: tests/function/test_template.py, test_bare.py and test_lifted.py, plus the typing checks.
  • Mutation checks: 56 mutants across the phases. 53 are killed, two of them only after a new test. Of the other three, two exposed dead code, which is removed, and one is equivalent: a .concrete call that only narrows the static type.
  • Call overhead (perf_2026_09_27_templates/, µs per call): one leaf 2.83 → 2.45; a group of four 5.81 → 5.07; four parameters 4.48; a template with one hole 3.20.
  • ty: the same diagnostics as before the branch, none new. The C snapshots, the smoke benchmarks, the CasADi comparisons (17 of 17) and the notebooks are unchanged.
