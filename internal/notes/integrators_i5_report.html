Integrators · I5 report

API-146 · collocation and pseudospectral transcriptions · branch claude/integrators-mpc · 27 Sep 2026 · plan integrators_mpc_plan_2026_09_27.md

Summary. The contract scaly.mpc builds horizons from. A transcription turns a model and a running cost into one interval Function, (x, u, [z], xnext, *params, [dt]) → [residuals; cost], which a horizon maps over its intervals.

  • Three transcriptions.
    • MultipleShooting wraps any integrator, and integrates the cost as one more state.
    • Collocation, at Radau or Gauss points, equals Radau IIA and Gauss-Legendre to 1e-12 at a fixed control.
    • Pseudospectral is Radau pseudospectral as in GPOPS-II, with controls at the nodes. Its error falls from 4 to 12 nodes faster than any fixed order.
  • Found on the way, a slow core path. Every NLP with more than one variable leaf fell off the structured sparse-Jacobian path. ad/sparse.py now accepts slices of the variable and differentiates all variable blocks in one pass. Horizon Jacobians:
    • Radau collocation: 19.2 → 2.1 µs;
    • pseudospectral: 71.9 → 4.4 µs;
    • shooting: 6.9 → 4.8 µs, now 0.56× CasADi SX's time on I1's cart-pole horizon.
  • Changed from the plan. Lobatto collocation and the LGL and LG pseudospectral schemes were dropped (§4).

Suite: 3 069 passed, 42 skipped, 52 xfailed. The one failure is Colin's uncommitted tiny_qp test. ruff and ty are clean on every path touched. Mutation checks: 32 of 32 killed. Two needed a test first: an equivalent-at-degree-≥2 mutant pinned at degree 1, and a parameter-order mutant.

2. Tests and mutation checks

  • Polynomials. The differentiation and interpolation matrices are exact on polynomials up to the node count, for three node sets and three sizes. Differentiation rows sum to zero, and interpolation at a node is exact.
  • Collocation.
    • Radau and Gauss collocation of degrees 2, 3 and 4, solved by SciPy, equal the implicit step of the same nodes to 1e-12.
    • Layouts: the variable and residual counts, and the input names.
  • Costs.
    • Quadrature is exact for a quadratic cost on a linear trajectory, for every scheme.
    • Degree-1 Radau is the right-endpoint rule.
    • Shooting integrates its cost with RK4, against DOP853.
    • The implicit shooting residual equals si.implicit's.
  • Pseudospectral.
    • Spectral convergence: the error at 12 nodes is below 1e-7, and 8 nodes improve on 4 by more than 100×.
    • Node controls act at their nodes: with a ramped control under x' = u, the end state and the node states are exact.
    • The layout.
  • Other.
    • A dt input equals the folded interval, on a model with a parameter, so the order params, dt is pinned.
    • A horizon of intervals: the sparse Jacobian equals the dense one on its pattern, and the dense one equals finite differences.
    • The refusals.
  • Structured path (tests/ad/test_sparsity.py).
    • The _window_offset cases.
    • A map over four runs of one variable gives exactly one derivative map, a coloring width equal to the sum of the local colors, and values equal to the dense Jacobian.

4. Changed from the plan

  • Lobatto collocation is gone. Lobatto points include the start. A polynomial through the states at the points is then one degree short of the collocation conditions, and the system is overdetermined. Lobatto IIIA remains available as an integrator, si.implicit.
  • LGL pseudospectral is gone. It collocates at both ends, one condition more than its unknowns at a fixed control; the known defect of the Legendre pseudospectral method.
  • LG pseudospectral is gone. It has no node at the interval's start, so no control there for a receding horizon to apply. Gauss points remain available as Collocation(points="legendre").
  • No Chebyshev nodes. Nothing here needs them.
