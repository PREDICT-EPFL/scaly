Interpolation · SP5 report

API-175 · shape-constrained fitting · branch claude/interp · 28 Sep 2026 · plan interp_plan_2026_09_28.md

Summary. interp.constrained fits a B-spline to scattered data by least squares under constraints that hold everywhere, not only at the data. It is solved as a QP by PIQP when the graph is built.

  • Constraints: monotone and convex/concave per axis, bounds, pinned values and derivatives, periodic ends. Each is a sufficient linear condition on the coefficients: on the derivative spline's coefficients, or on the coefficients themselves by the convex-hull property. A 2-D map can be monotone in one axis and bounded.
  • A polish after the interior point. PIQP stops with every inactive constraint still pushing by its barrier, which left a fit whose constraints were all slack 3.5e-10 from least squares. The fix solves the least squares once more, with the active set held as equalities, and keeps the result when it is feasible and every multiplier has the right sign. Fits then match least squares to rounding and sit on their bounds exactly.
  • Two fixes to the core sparsity code, which the 2-D fits exposed. A 16×16 map's first call took 56 s building its solver: the matmul pattern and the star-colouring recovery walked every triple and every neighbour in Python. Both are now sparse products, and that first call takes 6.4 s.
  • Cost: a solver is generated once per problem size, 0.5 to 2 s in 1-D. After that a fit takes 1.4 to 9 ms in 1-D and 12 to 144 ms in 2-D; BVLS matches it on bounds alone, and trust-constr is 50 to 90× slower.

Suite: 2 894 passed, 45 skipped (full suite, run after the change to the AD sparsity code). ruff and ty clean. Mutation checks: 27 of 27 killed, 19 on the fit and 8 on the sparsity code; three after a test change, one planned mutant replaced as equivalent.

1. What changed

PieceDetails
interp/constrained.py (new)
  • constrained(x, y, *, degree, knots, weights, monotone, convex, bounds, equal, periodic, penalty, lam, extrap, fill, search, strategy, dtype, name) returns a BSpline with constant coefficients.
  • Knots: clamped, over the data's range per axis; knots sets the number of equal intervals, or gives the interior knots.
  • Objective: half the squared residual of M c − target, where M stacks the weighted design matrix and √lam times the penalty-th differences along each axis (Kronecker products over the coefficient tensor). Its Hessian MᵀM is the QP's.
  • Monotone rows: de Boor's differencing matrix, derivative_matrix. Convex rows: that matrix applied twice, the second on the derivative's knots. Both act along one axis.
  • Bounds: the identity. Pinned values and derivatives: rows of the tensor basis's mixed derivatives. Periodic: the value and k − 1 derivatives equal at both ends.
  • One PIQP solver per (n, n_eq, n_ineq), built from sc.qp_problem with every tolerance at 1e-12 and cached for the process. A status other than OK or ACCEPTABLE raises and names the status. Infeasible constraints are the usual cause.
  • _polish: the active set is the constraints whose multiplier exceeds their slack (or whose slack is at rounding). The equality-constrained least squares is solved by a null-space basis from an SVD of the active rows and lstsq on M N, so the condition number is never squared. It is kept only if it is feasible to 1e-9 and each multiplier (lstsq of Cᵀ λ = Mᵀ r) has the sign of a bound that holds the fit back.
ad/sparsity.py_matmul_mask: out[i, j] depends on row i of x and column j of y. Each operand's K pattern rows are ORed by one sparse product with kron(I, 1ᵀ), then repeated with kron(·, 1). It used to enumerate m·n·K triples in Python.
ad/sparse.py_star_recovery_indices: the neighbours of each vertex in each colour come from one product, the adjacency times one-hot colours. Each Hessian entry looks up its two counts with searchsorted. Same result and same error. It used to walk a set of neighbours per entry.
Wiringinterp.constrained exported. IMPORT_LAYERS gets scaly.interp.constrained at layer 5 (it imports scaly.solvers, also 5). The public surface is pinned.
