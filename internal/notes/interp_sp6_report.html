API-176 · notebooks and CasADi pairs · branch claude/interp · 28 Sep 2026 · plan interp_plan_2026_09_28.md

Summary. Six executed notebooks in examples/interp/, five CasADi pairs in examples/interp/pairs/, and compare.py --dir.

  • The notebooks end in assertion cells against SciPy, papers, CasADi, trust-constr, finite differences, KKT conditions or an adaptive integrator. tests/integration/test_notebooks.py runs them, which takes 30 s with the JIT cache warm.
  • The pairs all agree. The only gap above 10⁻¹³ is a 2·10⁻¹⁰ bicubic Hessian, which comes from CasADi's own fit. Both sides take the same number of IPOPT iterations in every pair.
  • Run times: Scaly is 2.5× to 860× faster than CasADi's virtual machine, and 2× to 420× faster than CasADi with its oracles compiled.
  • No library code changed. The work found seven things for later, listed in §5. Among them: CasADi's parametric interpolants have a zero derivative in their data unless inlined, and its bspline is zero outside its grid; Scaly repeats a constant table in every generated function that reads it (C-187, new); and auto misses a faster search on small axes in batches.

Suite: 2 900 passed, 45 skipped. ruff and ty clean on the pairs and the changed files. Mutation checks: 5 of 5 killed by the notebooks' own checks.

5. Found along the way

  • C-187 (new, open). A constant table is emitted once per generated function that reads it. contouring_control's law repeats its 5 440-value track table six times, 941 kB of C.
  • The auto search on small axes at random batch points (for the review round). On a 10 × 11 table, count beats the binary that auto picks by about 0.5 ns a point. SP4 tuned auto on one repeated point, where count never won.
  • CasADi, pinned in the README:
    • the derivative in a parametric interpolant's data, or a bspline's symbolic coefficients, is zero unless inlined, and IPOPT then reports success at the initial point;
    • bspline is zero outside its grid, which a relaxed bound reaches;
    • lookup_mode="exact" is refused for bspline;
    • its JIT with expand=True fails on interpolant graphs.
  • An interpolating cubic's Jacobian in its data is dense even at known points, since its fit is global. learning_tables advises B-spline coefficients as the variables.
  • For the review round.
    • tests/interp/test_constrained.py's OCV "truth" falls near full charge. The test only checks that the fit is monotone, so it is not wrong, but its docstring's physics is.
    • BSpline's docstring still lists the searches as before SP4, without bucket.
    • inverse() accepted a fit whose derivative coefficients reach −4·10⁻¹⁴.
