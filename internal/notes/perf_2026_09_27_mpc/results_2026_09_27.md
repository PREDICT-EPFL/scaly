# Results, 2026-09-27

## M1: an OCP from `scaly.mpc` against the same problem by hand (`bench_ocp.py --rounds 15`)

The cart-pole of `tests/mpc/test_ocp.py`, N = 20, IPOPT at `tol = 1e-8`, one cold start repeated;
the fastest of 15 interleaved rounds.

| variant | IPOPT iterations | IPOPT t_total ms | call wall ms | vs hand | build + compile s |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| hand | 23 | 3.273 | 3.305 | 1.000 | 1.08 |
| library | 23 | 3.190 | 3.259 | 0.974 | 1.06 |
| library_collocation3 | 23 | 5.901 | 6.013 | 1.803 | 0.61 |
| library_pseudospectral4 | 68 | 31.528 | 31.696 | 9.632 | 1.05 |

`library` builds the same NLP (the same variables in the same order, the same constraints) and
IPOPT takes the same path. The collocation and pseudospectral rows are context: more variables, and
for pseudospectral controls free inside each interval, which the soft track limit, imposed at the grid
points only, lets it use.
