# Stored-coordinate derivatives

Issue #46 keeps sparse tangents on the primal's stored pattern. Their storage is
`(nseed, nnz)` per matrix, with shared constant index tables across seed kernels.
The existing elementwise partial table evaluates on stored coordinates. A product
with one sparse operand keeps that operand sparse in forward mode. Reverse mode
samples the cotangent for its sparse adjoint and scatters stored contributions for
the dense adjoint. It does not form a dense outer product or project the sparse
primal through a dense matrix. Sparse movement adjoints scatter into stored slots.

The structural product join in `ad/sparsity.py` already implements the required
matrix-product dependency rule. The workload gates pin it against devrush's
results. A perturbation replacing it with an empty relation fails the comparison.

The 48 Maros-Meszaros problem archives and LICENSE are copied unchanged from
`origin/devrush:src/scaly/testing/data/maros_meszaros/`. NOTICE retains the upstream
attribution and records the new references. Reference pattern
archives were generated by importing the frozen modules through `git show`,
without copying their implementation. `tests/integration/test_sparse_kkt.py`
rewrites the regularized Karush-Kuhn-Tucker system stated by
`src/scaly/opt/ipm/kkt.py::KKT`, exercised by
`internal/notes/perf_2026_09_26_tier3/t3_3_kkt.py`, with main's public API. Numerical
references come independently from SciPy and central differences. All 48 KKT
patterns, KKT-value Jacobians and five product Jacobians agree with devrush.
Factoring and solving the systems, and sparse-sparse products, remain outside
this task.

## Gates and perturbations

- Restoring either old dense projection fails the banded sparse-leaf memory gate
  at n=100, with a 10,000-entry private buffer. The corrected gradient and sparse
  Hessian also pass at n=400, including SciPy values and finite differences.
- Changing the sampled vector adjoint's coefficient fails both product directions.
- Replacing multi-seed sparse gather tangents with zero fails all four region
  derivative gates. Returning zero from forward differentiation fails all 17
  kernel and region finite-difference cases.
- Disabling the shared row-pointer table produces five copies and fails the
  multiseed gate. The correct lowering emits one.
- Allocating sparse intermediates by mathematical shape fails the KKT memory gate.
- Corrupting a saved devrush Jacobian coordinate fails the pattern comparison.

Every perturbation was removed. Dense C snapshots stay unchanged. Several seeds
continue to share one pattern; per-entry seed lists and compaction remain #225.
Derivative outputs retain their existing compact values and separate pattern,
which #48 changes. SELECT integration waits for #320's operation on this base.
