O5: a tile is one loop

Step 5 of the generated-code speed plan · todo C-200 · branch perf/codegen-speed · 30 Sep 2026 · Apple M3 Max, Apple clang 21, the JIT's flags

Summary. Multi-seed forward mode stacks a primal factor once per seed (stack([d] * nseed)), and lowering gave every copy its own loop, which fusion cannot inline since the buffer is written by many loops: 42 loops of three elements at chain M = 9, four of 32 in the MLP Jacobian, four of 246 in the race-car Hessian. A stack or concat along axis 0 of one input repeated now lowers to one loop, out[i] = src[i % size], which fusion inlines into its consumer and delinearize_loops then splits into nested loops without the remainder.

  • Speed: mlp_small_jac 1.13× faster; the race cars 0.92–0.99; chain M = 5 and M = 9, npmpc and unbumpercars unchanged within noise (chain M = 9 timed 746 µs in both builds by a direct A/B; one 15-round run read 1.09 on a low outlier of the old build).
  • Code: chain M = 9's entry has 110 loops instead of 278; no workspace size changes.
  • Tests: 4 430 passed, 57 skipped, 5 xfailed (2 new, one updated). Five mutants, five killed.
  • Undo: git revert the commit “Codegen speed O5”.
