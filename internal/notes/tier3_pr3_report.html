Tier 3 · PR 3 report

T3-3 · the KKT backends (C-127) · branch claude/tier3-ipm · 26 Sep 2026

Summary. scaly.solvers.ipm.KKT is PIQP's KKTSystem as generated code.

  • Shared by both backends: the box and inequality barrier regularizations, the reduced right-hand sides, and the recovery of duals and slacks for one- and two-sided rows.
  • Dense backend: the condensed matrix P + X + AᵀA/δ + Gᵀ Z⁻¹ G is assembled from sparse products, densified once and Cholesky-factored.
  • Sparse backend: the upper triangle of the whole KKT matrix, factored by SparseLDL.
  • Exact. Solves match the reference to 1e-7 for two regularization levels on 10 stored problems, a free-row problem, MPC and a problem with two-sided rows, on both backends. Factor.ok reports a failed factorization as a non-finite pivot.
  • Left for Tier 4: iterative refinement and factorization retries (#29), as planned.

Mutation checks: 9 of 9 killed, one only after the two-sided case was added.
