What was implemented

  • Per-step Jacobian tangents (fef21de, _jvp_many_compressed).
    • A step with m seeded input entries and more seeds than that forms its m-column Jacobian once per step. The seeds in that pass are constant unit vectors, so it folds to the nonzero partials. The tangents are then seeds @ J.
    • Above 256 multiply-adds per step, the primal and the varying Jacobians move to a straight-line callee (*_stepjac*). The product becomes a block-lowered map over the seeds of a small callee, so the body is one loop over the seeds with its accumulators in registers.
    • I chose this instead of the agent's hoisted seed loop. It reaches the same effect with Expr rewrites only, and the step's seed-independent work is done once per step by construction.
  • C-91 (e2723bd). Reverse mode groups the output nodes of one scan, and of one call, and differentiates them together when the last one is reached. The grouping had to include calls: your cost reads X and costs through shoot's two outputs, and each output used to get its own sweep through the callee.
  • Index maps (b418022).
    • A new Program pass, delinearize_loops, splits a flat loop whose indices divide the loop variable into nested loops, along the finest factorization in which every index is affine.
    • Expr simplification composes gather∘gather, turns a scatter that writes every entry once into a gather, and folds a transpose into a gather.
    • The Hessian's final assembly is now one loop writing res directly:
    for (i0 = 0; i0 < 40; ++i0) for (i1 = 0; i1 < 40; ++i1)
  res[0][40*i0 + i1] = s9[1560 - 40*i0 + i1];            // was: zero 1600, scatter with /40, transposed copy
  • JIT (7ccd81a). The entry point takes c_void_p addresses, and the pointer-array types are built once. A call now leaves no reference cycles.
