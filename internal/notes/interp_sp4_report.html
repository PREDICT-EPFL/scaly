Interpolation · SP4 report

API-174 · interpolation performance · branch claude/interp · 28 Sep 2026 · plan interp_plan_2026_09_28.md

Summary. Measured, then tuned; nothing in the gate calls for a dedicated op.

  • A bucket search: a uniform bucket index, the bucket's first cell from a table, then as many compares as the fullest bucket holds (at most four). Clustered 1 024-site tables drop from 5.4 to 1.9 ns (linear) and 5.8 to 3.1 ns (cubic); on uniform grids it beats the uniform search too. auto: binary up to 32 cells, then bucket, uniform, binary; within 5 % of the best fixed choice everywhere but one row that compares two builds of the same variant.
  • The default linear extrapolation, tabulated: two cells beyond each end hold the end tangents as polynomials, so it costs a lookup instead of a clamp and a slope at every point (bicubic: 11.7 to 8.2 ns).
  • Thresholds by measurement: DENSE_FIT 256 → 40 (the scans win from ~40 sites), PP_BUDGET 1 → 4 MB (per-cell polynomials stay faster even at 8 MB, but cost 14× the C), a warning at 2²⁰ tabulated values. Uniform cells compute their centers (the 1e6-point table: 84 → 63 MB of C).
  • Deployment: float32 tables and evaluation (to 1e-6), BSpline.pack, tables as inputs compiled from a C main, and the header states the shape and order of multi-axis buffers.
  • Against CasADi, now with its bspline node and blazing_spline too: 0.03× to 0.09× its fastest per point in 1-D and 2-D, 0.30× on the 3-D trilinear; batches 25× faster than its map; SciPy's legacy cubic grid interpolator costs 32 µs a point.

Suite: 2 873 passed, 45 skipped. ruff and ty clean. Mutation checks: 12 of 12 killed, one after the bucket search stopped making a compare no bucket needed.

  • Batches of random points (bench_batch.py): 3.0 to 3.3 ns a point in 1-D and 6.3 to 7.0 in 2-D for 10³ to 10⁶ points, against 60 to 82 and 121 to 129 for CasADi's map.
  • Large tables (bench_codegen.py): a 10⁶-point linear table is 63 MB of C and 2.8 s to compile (CasADi: 49 MB, 1.8 s); the 256² bicubic as local bases 1.5 MB and 0.1 s; the 64³ tricubic 5.4 MB, where CasADi spends 15 s fitting it.
  • The inverse costs about 17 evaluations (54 ns); left as is.
  • Not done: hint search. With the bucket and uniform searches at one to two nanoseconds, a search from a carried index could save less than that, so it was not built.
