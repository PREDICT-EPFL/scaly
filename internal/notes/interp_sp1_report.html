Interpolation · SP1 report

API-171 · scaly.interp: tables and interpolating splines · branch claude/interp · 28 Sep 2026 · plan interp_plan_2026_09_28.md

Summary. A new sub-package, scaly.interp, at import layer 5. Every interpolant is one object, a tensor-product B-spline (interp.BSpline), evaluated as generated code built from existing ops (floor, cast, take, where, arithmetic). No new IR op.

  • interp.interpolant(grid, values, kind=...): nearest, zoh, linear, cubic (not-a-knot, natural, clamped, periodic) and spline of degree 1 to 5, per axis, in 1-D to 4-D, with vector and matrix outputs. Values equal SciPy's to 1e-13 relative, first and second derivatives (forward and reverse) to 1e-11.
  • Five extrapolation modes and NaN in, NaN out in every mode. Three searches, each equal to searchsorted on more than 10⁶ adversarial points. Two strategies: per-cell polynomials (pp) or local bases (basis).
  • Against CasADi's generated interpolant, one point per call: 0.04× to 0.15× its time in 1-D and 2-D and 0.29× on a 3-D trilinear table, with a sixth to a tenth of the C.

Suite: 2 769 passed, 45 skipped (SP0: 2 652). ruff clean; ty clean on src/ and the new tests. Mutation checks: 21 of 22 killed; the survivor was dead code, now removed.

1. What was built

PieceDetails
interp/grid.py
  • Axis: knot vector, degree, a search partition (every distinct knot of the base interval, refinable by a fit's data sites so a uniform data grid stays uniform when not-a-knot drops two knots), cell continuity (right, or left for nearest's midpoints), extrapolation.
  • Searches as expressions: uniform (floor, clamp before the cast, a one-step correction against the cell's own two edges), binary (branch-free halvings on edges padded with NaN, which every compare fails, so x = +inf cannot run past the last cell), count. auto takes uniform when every edge is within a quarter cell of uniform, else binary.
  • The per-axis B-spline tables, by de Boor differencing on sparse design matrices: the local polynomial of every non-zero basis function (local), the first basis function per cell (offsets), and the piecewise-polynomial form of coefficients (taylor). Local expansions are centred on the cell midpoints, which halves the cancellation of a left-edge expansion: a 2-D cubic missed 1e-13 with left edges and meets it centred.
interp/spline.py
  • BSpline(knots, coeffs, degree, extrap=, fill=, search=, strategy=, name=). A point is evaluated inline; a batch (N,) or (N, D) is one vmap of the point's Function. The first version evaluated batches as one vectorized graph, and reverse mode through its middle-axis slices grew with N (8 424 C lines for a 49-point Hessian, 45 s to build); the map is 399 lines at any N, and sparse_jacobian takes its structured path.
  • pp: the cell's prod(k_d+1) Taylor coefficients by one take, nested Horner. basis: per axis the local matrix by one take and Horner, the prod(k_d+1) B-coefficients by one take, a matvec per axis. auto: pp while its table fits 1 MB.
  • Extrapolation: linear (default from degree 2; in n-D the product of the axes' linear continuations, which is C¹, where the plan's plain Taylor formula is not across the lines a point leaves a second axis), extend (what linear is at degree 1), clamp (the default at degree 0), periodic, fill. The clamp uses where, so NaN reaches the output; degree-0 axes, which never read their coordinate, pass NaN on explicitly.
  • index(x) and f(x, index=) share one search (for a batch, a map of a search Function). function() is interned by a content digest, so identical splines share one Function and never collide at lowering. to_scipy().
interp/fit.pyinterpolant: per axis the knot vector, partition and a linear map from data to coefficients (identity for nearest, zoh, linear; make_interp_spline for cubic and spline), applied axis by axis. Periodic axes check that their data close up. zoh holds its last value for one more spacing, or to period.
Wiringsc.interp (no flat re-exports), IMPORT_LAYERS, the pinned surface in test_import_boundaries.py, a table entry in the C snapshot corpus (a 5-point linear table: 57 lines).

2. Tests and mutation checks

tests/interp/ (98 tests, 21 s in parallel), plus tests/integration/test_interp_ocp.py and tests/codegen/test_constant_tables.py:

  • test_grid.py: every search against searchsorted on 10⁶ points per search and side (5 × 10⁵ for uniform, which skips the non-uniform partitions): each edge, nextafter on both sides, ±10³ widths, NaN, ±inf, ±1e300, random, over six partitions; uniformity on 60 linspace grids; the correction on edges off by 0.45 cell; the de Boor tables against SciPy, knot multiplicities included.
  • test_spline.py: degrees 0 to 5 in 1-D and five degree mixes in 2-D to 4-D against SciPy's BSpline/NdBSpline, both strategies, values and first and second derivatives in forward and reverse mode; each extrapolation mode against its formula (the 2-D tensor continuation included); NaN and ±inf per mode and degree; the shared search (one floor in the C of value, gradient and Hessian); batches as one map with a block-diagonal sparse Jacobian.
  • test_fit.py: np.interp, CubicSpline for all four boundary conditions (periodic extrapolation included), make_interp_spline k = 1 to 5, RGI nearest away from midpoints and the lower site at a float midpoint, searchsorted for zoh, 2-D and 3-D tensor fits, a 4-D mixed table interpolating its data, validation.
  • test_casadi_parity.py: linear inside and extended, 1-D to 3-D, to 1e-13; bspline evaluation through CasADi's bspline node with our knots and coefficients, k = 1, 3, 5, 1-D to 3-D, to 1e-13; CasADi's quirks pinned (zero outside, zero data derivative unless inlined, degrees 2 and 4 refused).
  • An IPOPT OCP whose dynamics contain a bicubic table solves as the same OCP with the table written out as comparisons and polynomials, to 1e-8. Constant tables reach the C bit for bit.

CasADi's fit is the less accurate one. Its bspline interpolant misses its own data by up to 1.6e-10 (3-D, degree 5) where SciPy's per-axis solves miss by 4e-14, so the fitted interpolants agree to 1e-13 or to ten times CasADi's miss, and the test asserts ours is the smaller. The plan's "1e-13 in 3-D" holds for the evaluation, not for CasADi's fit.
