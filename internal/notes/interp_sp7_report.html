docs/guide/interp.md (new)

It covers the plan's list:

  • the kinds table, with continuity and the SciPy or CasADi object each one matches;
  • the five extrapolation modes;
  • evaluation: points and batches, search, strategy, index;
  • derivatives and the Jacobian's pattern at known against symbolic points, with the interpolating cubic's dense pattern and the advice that follows from it;
  • where coefficients come from: interpolation, smoothing, shape constraints, your own, data at run time;
  • calculus;
  • generated code: the large-table warning, a table as an input with its header comment and pack, float32, function(), and the per-function table copies of C-187;
  • a section for CasADi users: data order, the zero derivative without inline, the zero outside, degrees and lookup.
