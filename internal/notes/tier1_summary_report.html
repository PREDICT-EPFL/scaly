Independent review

A separate agent reviewed the finished branch, trying to break it with experiments. It found four bugs that tests at the time did not catch:

SeverityBugFix
highThe in-place rule computed the read set of later updates against a chain link, which derivative patterns do not treat as a variable, so it came out empty. A body that swaps two entries was accepted and gave [10, 1, 1, 3] instead of [10, 2, 1, 3].The values are cut at the link and the pattern is taken against a stand-in symbol.
highDerivative patterns omit reads that have no derivative, such as copysign's sign. An accepted body gave [3, 5] instead of [3, −5].Read sets are only trusted through an explicit list of operations whose pattern equals their reads. Any other operation on the path to the carry is treated as reading every entry.
highA custom_derivative copy kept its Function's name, so derivative helpers keyed by name were shared. With fn and its copy in one graph, the result was 12 or 120 instead of 66.The copy gets its own name, derived from its rules.
mediumCustom rules were ignored when the Function was a scan or while_loop body.Every derivative that looks inside a Function now goes through body_tangents/body_cotangents.
lowNext to a NaN operand, the derivative of maximum/minimum went to the operand fmax discards.The weight follows the operand C returns.

Two plausible issues were also hardened:

  • The in-place chain is now recomputed on the exact graph being lowered.
  • The while_loop tangent cache is keyed by the condition object instead of its id.

The reviewer also checked, and confirmed correct, a wide set of other cases: scan lowering (lengths 0–4, shared outputs, nesting, scan inside vmap), scan AD with rank-2 carries and negative and zero strides (including reverse over reverse), while_loop AD and Hessians, and the bool ABI.
