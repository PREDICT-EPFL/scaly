Function templates P6: documentation, examples, cleanup

2026-09-27 · branch claude/function-templates · plan v2, step P6

Summary. The functions guide is rewritten from the shortest declaration (@sc.function) to the most specific, and every code block on it runs. The other pages that taught the old convention now use the new one. 49 example files (scripts, the CasADi comparisons and notebooks) are in the new style, and their printed results and the CasADi agreement are unchanged. The conversions surfaced one API gap, now closed: a name-only output (output="K") takes a SparseMatrix result.

Documentation

  • docs/guide/functions.md, rewritten. It covers a first function, then symbolic and numerical calls, one body many shapes, and declaring shapes (full and partial). Then names, structured arguments and results, dtypes, and sparse matrices. Then differentiating, composing, the three loop builders (their code updated), ahead of time, a declaration table, and inspection. A harness ran all 16 runnable blocks in order, and a second script checked the stated instance names, refusals and C symbols.
  • getting_started.md (functions, derivatives and vmap sections; runs end to end with the IPOPT solve), derivatives.md (one name is wrt, defaults, derivatives of templates), codegen.md (instances as AOT targets, the CLI), sparsity.md, solvers.md, installation.md, how_it_works/architecture.md (the two classes), how_it_works/ir.md, docs/dev/codebase.md, and the README and docs/index.md quickstart: @sc.function(2, 1, 2) def defect(z, u, znext).
  • typing_playground/README.md says the sketches are the single-tree design and why multi-parameter bodies are no longer deferred; its open items shrink to what remains.
  • refactorings.md loses its "Function templates" section; its zero-input section records the settled dispatch.
  • The docs site builds with no issues.

What stayed declared, deliberately:

  • every vmap/scan callee;
  • every function compiled at a fixed shape or written to C;
  • names that the CasADi counterparts or printed text depend on.

Bare @sc.function went to symbolic helpers and while_loop callees. Those callees' C names now spell their shapes (line_search_step__123_sint64_4_4_240_120_600_3 in the iLQR notebook); the exported names are unchanged. Some private _from_exprs builders in notebooks became decorated functions.

Found on the way

  • A name-only output could not be sparse. output="K" is a leaf whose shape the trace decides, but a body returning a SparseMatrix was refused. Now L.infer lets a whole-hole leaf take the kind the body returns, through the leaf_tree hook. There is a new test and two killed mutants.
  • tests/linalg/test_examples.py called a converted example with one tuple; fixed.
  • ty refuses a Python float or np.float64 where a leaf's numerical type is np.ndarray, though the runtime coerces them. This predates the templates. Examples keep np.array(...) around scalars; it is recorded in API-80.
