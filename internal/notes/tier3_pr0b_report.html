Tier 3 · PR 0b report

T3-0b · the NumPy reference of PIQP 0.6.2 (C-124) · branch claude/tier3-ipm · 26 Sep 2026

Summary. The Tier 3 oracle exists and passes its gate.

  • A line-by-line port of PIQP 0.6.2 (tests/solvers/ipm/reference.py, NumPy and SciPy): the solver loop, Ruiz equilibration, preprocessing, and the KKT system with iterative refinement. It solves the same regularized KKT matrix as PIQP's sparse backend, with SuperLU instead of an unpivoted LDLᵀ.
  • Gate met. Decision traces (status, iteration count, and ρ and δ at every iteration to 1e-3) match PIQP's sparse backend on 46 of 48 stored problems; the threshold is 90%. They also match on every problem where PIQP's own two backends follow the same path. The two misses, QCAPRI and QSHARE2B, are rounding-sensitive: PIQP's backends drift apart on them as well.
  • Full precision. Iterates agree to 1e-13 after the first iteration on every problem. Traces agree to about 1e-14 early and 1e-8 to 1e-5 near convergence, where the KKT system is ill-conditioned. With iterative refinement always on, 48 of 48 match.
  • PIQP quirks found and reproduced:
    • The box terms of the primal residual and of the primal proximal infeasibility enter as a signed max, so a negative box residual never counts.
    • Cost scaling computes its maxima in the memory that the Ruiz stopping test reads for the box scaling.
    • 0/0 in the μ-rate and in σ gives NaN, which std::max and std::min map to 0 and 1.
    Each quirk is pinned by a test that fails without it.
  • Plan revised (v4.5). The proximal ρ/δ updates, the boundary shift and infeasibility detection move from Tier 4 into T3-4, because matching the reference needs them.

Mutation checks: 23 mutants of the decision rules and the scaling, all killed. Nine were killed only after tests were added: a stability-filtered per-problem check, scaling edge cases, and two targeted problems for the boundary shifts.

PieceDetails
Data, setup_dataPIQP's preprocessing. Bounds at or beyond ±1e30 are absent. A row infinite on both sides keeps its place with its G row zeroed and bounds [−1, 1]. Finite box bounds are packed at the head, with their indices.

One trace row per iteration in PIQP's table layout, plus σ.
problems.random_qpRandom feasible QPs and LPs. Several of the LPs are unbounded, and some degenerate ones run PIQP to its iteration limit, which is useful for Tier 4.

2. Tests and mutation checks

tests/solvers/ipm/test_reference.py, 133 cases, 14 of them skipped as rounding-sensitive:

  • the gate;
  • full precision on HS21, DUAL1, HS118 and TAME;
  • agreement to rounding after one iteration on all 48;
  • refinement always on;
  • cost scaling, including the aliasing case;
  • MPC traces;
  • infeasible statuses;
  • KKT and complementarity of every reference solution;
  • free rows, the signed maxima and the no-inequality branch;
  • decisions on every problem PIQP's backends agree on (MM, infeasible, MPC, random);
  • scaling edge cases (tiny and huge coefficients, tiny rows) at full precision;
  • the inequality-dual shift.

Killed mutants: σ squared; τ missing from the predictor; the damped ρ factor; the early-iteration window; the 5% decrease test; box residuals by absolute value; the projection radius; no μ floor; each Ruiz limit; the Ruiz stopping tolerance; free rows bounded by 2; each boundary shift; no fine-tuning; no static regularization; infeasibility after one miss; no gap check; the centre never moving; no aliasing; the equality-only ρ factor; the box barrier missing from the x regularization; the sign in the two-sided dual recovery.
