T3-1 · loop-invariant while_loop inputs (C-112) · branch claude/tier3-ipm · 26 Sep 2026

Summary. sc.while_loop(cond, body, init, max_iter=…, params=(…)) passes tensors that every step reads unchanged to the body and the condition where they are, instead of copying them into the carry. The IPM's outer loop needs this: the problem data, scalings and factor inputs can stay out of its carry.

  • IR. The params are extra arguments of the WHILE node, and an explicit index attribute replaces inference from the body's input count. The spec rule checks every param against both functions.
  • Lowering. Both calls receive views of the params' buffers, and the params join the loop's identity key. An int64 constant param is a table the in-place proof can read.
  • AD. In forward mode (single- and multi-seed), the tangent loop takes the params' tangents as further params. In reverse mode, the backward scan reads the params as stride-0 inputs and sums their cotangents in its carry over the steps actually taken. Sparsity brings the params' pattern in at every step.
  • First user. SparseLDL's adaptive refinement carries [x | r] only, and still updates it in place. Its overhead over a plain solve falls by 12–45%.

Suite: all tests pass. Mutation checks: 11 of 11 killed.

1. Tests and mutation checks

tests/integration/test_while_params.py, 11 tests:

  • values, Jacobians, gradients, Hessians and jvp through the params, compared with the unrolled loop to 1e-12, with and without the step number;
  • only params that depend on wrt get cotangents;
  • sparsity;
  • no buffer of a large param's size in the C;
  • the in-place proof reading an integer constant param;
  • the refinement loop's shape and its in-place procedure;
  • two loops that differ only in their params, in one graph;
  • a constant carry still differentiable through its params;
  • validation, the verifier and the printer.

Killed mutants: the loop key without params; no integer table for the proof; forward and multi-seed forward dropping the params' tangents; reverse accumulating untaken steps; reverse ignoring the params; sparsity without the params; the spec skipping param shapes; the output's diff flag from the initial carry only; the constructor skipping param checks; the printer dropping params.
